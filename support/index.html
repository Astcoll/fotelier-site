Support

Questions, bugs or feedback

Email support@fotelier.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Where are my edits? Finished edits are kept in the Fotelier library inside the app. If you turned on saving to Photos, a copy is also in your photo library.

Will I lose my edits if I delete the app? Yes, unless you restore the app from an iPhone backup. The library lives only on your device. Copies you saved to Photos stay in Photos.
