Fotelier privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Fotelier edits photos on your device. It works offline, makes no network requests, and has no accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising. We do not collect, receive or sell your photos or personal information.

You choose which photos to import with the system photo picker. Fotelier keeps local copies, your edits and recipes, and any capture date or camera details used for frames. If you choose to save a finished edit to Photos, the app asks for permission to add it. You can change that permission in your device settings.

You can delete an edit from the Fotelier library. Your originals and any copies you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there. If you share a photo, you choose the destination in the system share sheet. Device backups, photo syncing and the services you choose to share with follow their own settings.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed to handle your request.

For privacy questions, contact support@fotelier.app.
